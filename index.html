FAQ

frequently asked

what is pebble?+

a focus companion for mac. it lives in your notch, holds you to one goal and one app per sprint, and nudges you back when you drift.

is pebble watching my screen?+

during a session it reads active window titles (accessibility) and your active chrome tab (automation). screen recording is optional and only used if you turn on visual checks.

who decides if my reason is good enough?+

an ai evaluator. you write a concrete reason, it approves or turns it down, and an approved reason gets a sticky-note pass.

i code in the terminal. will pebble yell at me?+

turn on the claude code companion and terminal, ghostty, iterm2, warp, cursor and vs code are all allowed next to your main app.

does it work on windows?+

not yet. pebble is mac only for now.
